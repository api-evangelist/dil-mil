# Dil Mil

> Dil Mil is a South Asian dating and matchmaking platform that helps singles find meaningful, culturally-grounded connections. Its mobile app offers matching algorithms, interest-based community chat rooms, global matching, and a VIP Elite membership tier. Tagline: "Find Something Real." Dil Mil is a consumer mobile application with no public developer or API surface.
